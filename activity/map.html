Map Guide

Modes
↖ SelectMove and resize placed markers. This is the default mode.
✋ PanClick and drag to move around the map.
✏ DrawDraw freehand lines on the map.
➡ ArrowDraw directional arrows to show movement paths.
Placing Objectives & Players
DragDrag any objective icon or player chip from the sidebar onto the map to place it.
MoveIn Select mode, click and drag any placed marker to reposition it.
ResizeIn Select mode, hover over a marker and scroll up/down to make it bigger or smaller.
✕Hover over a marker and click the red ✕ button to remove it.
Zoom & Navigation
ScrollScroll wheel to zoom in and out on the map.
PinchOn mobile, pinch with two fingers to zoom.
+ / −Use the + and − buttons in the toolbar to zoom step by step.
⌂ ResetClick ⌂ to fit the full map back into view.
Middle clickHold middle mouse button and drag to pan anytime, regardless of mode.
Drawing
ColorsPick a color from the sidebar before drawing.
SizeAdjust the brush size slider in the sidebar.
↶ UndoRemove the last drawn stroke.
Players
Sat / Sun tabsSwitch between Saturday and Sunday player lists. Players available both days appear in both tabs.
🛡 / ⚔Players are grouped by Defend and Attack teams inside each day tab.
Top / BotJungle players show their assigned lane (Top or Bottom).
Save & Load
📥 ExportSave your current drawings as a JSON file to your device.
📤 ImportLoad a previously saved strategy file back onto the map.
🧹 ClearRemove all drawings and placed markers from the map.
